BirdsEye privacy notice

BirdsEye is a personal app written by its owner (GitHub: cmd105) for his own and his family's use, on their own iPhones. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it reads

With the owner's consent, BirdsEye reads account balances and transactions from his own bank accounts, through Enable Banking Oy, a regulated account information service provider. Access is read-only. The app cannot move money.

Where the data lives

On the owner's iPhone only, encrypted on the device. There is no BirdsEye server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Who else sees it

Enable Banking and the bank, in order to provide the account data. Anthropic, only when the owner asks the app's built-in assistant a question, using his own account and only the figures needed to answer.

The redirect page

The page at /bank/ only hands the bank's reply back to the app on the phone. It does not read, store or send anything.

Deleting data

The app can erase everything it holds, and bank access can be withdrawn at any time in the bank's own app or website.

Contact

The owner, through the GitHub account cmd105.
